### Artefact 1: Why RCA Matters Strategically
**Source Title**

Root Cause Analysis: Process, Techniques, and Best Practices

**What This Source Says**

This article from The Business Analyst Job Description (2025) explains that Root Cause Analysis is more than just a technical tool. It is actually a way organisations think about problems.

The article makes an important point: RCA can work in three different areas:

•	Safety — stopping workplace accidents

•	Production — making manufacturing faster and better

•	Business processes — improving how work gets done

What I found interesting is that RCA changes how organisations respond to problems. Instead of just fixing the immediate issue (reactive), good organisations use RCA to stop problems before they happen (proactive). That is a big difference. The article explains that real root causes are usually hidden underneath the surface. If you only fix what you see, the problem comes back. You have to dig deeper (The Business Analyst Job Description, 2025, p. 1).
